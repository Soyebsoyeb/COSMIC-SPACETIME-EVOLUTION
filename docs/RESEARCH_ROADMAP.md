# Research Roadmap

Items below are **planned and not implemented** in version 0.1.0.

| Priority | Item | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Interface to CAMB or CLASS | Validate $\sigma_8$, $\theta_*$, $z_*$ and obtain $C_\ell$ |
| 2 | BBN network backend (PArthENoPE, AlterBBN or PRIMAT through the existing hook) | Compute D, He and Li abundances instead of identities only |
| 3 | Massive neutrinos | Remove the largest known systematic in $\sigma_8$ and equality |
| 4 | Verify transfer-function and recombination coefficients against the original papers | Convert "recalled" entries to "verified" |
| 5 | Helium recombination and a RECFAST/HyRec comparison | Reduce the $\theta_*$ offset |
| 6 | Preheating and non-perturbative reheating | Replace the constant-$\Gamma$ toy model |
| 7 | Multi-field and non-canonical inflation | Broaden the model-dependent layer |
| 8 | Loop-quantum-cosmology effective bounce toy model | Clearly labelled quantum-cosmology module |
| 9 | Interacting and modified-gravity dark energy | Wider future scenarios |
| 10 | N-body and halofit hooks | Non-linear structure |
| 11 | Likelihood or MCMC wrapper over background observables | Statistical model comparison |
| 12 | Bayesian treatment of parameter uncertainty (replace independent Gaussian Monte Carlo) | More honest uncertainty |

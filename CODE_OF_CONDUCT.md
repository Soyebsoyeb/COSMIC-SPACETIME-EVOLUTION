# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone who contributes, regardless of background or experience level. Contributors are expected to:

* be respectful and constructive, especially in scientific disagreement;
* critique results, methods and code, not people;
* accept corrections gracefully and credit others' work;
* keep discussion on topic and free of personal attacks, discriminatory language, or unwelcome advances.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and to individuals representing the project in public.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits and contributions that violate this code, and may restrict participation. Report concerns to the maintainers at [CONTACT EMAIL PLACEHOLDER]. Reports will be handled with discretion.
